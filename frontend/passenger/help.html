Frequently asked questions

How do I book a ride?

From the home screen, select your pickup and destination, choose a ride option and payment preference, then review the trip. Booking is a demo until the Django backend is connected.

Can I cancel a ride?

Ride cancellations and any applicable fees will be enabled when real bookings and cancellation policies are implemented.

How are fares calculated?

Prices currently shown are examples. Real fares will be calculated on the Django server using verified route and pricing data.

How can I pay?

Cash and online payment are shown as options. Actual online payments will require secure Paystack and Django integration.

How do I report a safety issue?

The Safety Center is currently a preview. This demo form does not reach a human responder. If immediate help is needed, contact emergency services directly.
